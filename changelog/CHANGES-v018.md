now the client strip at the top of the email card — from, subject A/B,
preheader, grey, small — the way an inbox shows them, and the way Prize

container carries a client strip as its first tagged module — from,
subject (A/B where the spec says two), preheader — inside the artefact
card, never as a card above it. The engine reads it like any other
section; the padlock sits on it. One Update is the worked example.
