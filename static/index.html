ABOUT.

Robots are brilliant at squirting words into well designed containers. So if you have a good story (and good containers) this should work really well.

If you need help deciding what to say or designing great containers, humans are the way to go. Hunch is pretty good at both.

Once you've nailed those, you can roll with a robot every day of the week. Just feed it, fix it and you'll be finished in a flash. Happy days.
