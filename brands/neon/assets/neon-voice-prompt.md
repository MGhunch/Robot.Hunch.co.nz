## Brand

Neon talks like someone already deep in the rabbit hole who can't stop pulling you in after them — not a guide with a list, but a fan mid-thought who just has to share it. The voice lives in the gap between plain mechanics and charged feeling: 'Crack some skulls and win', never 'Watch and win'. The reader lands curious, not informed — like there's something worth finding and they've just been pointed at the door.

## Who we are

- **Are:** The fan who's already three episodes in and can't not tell you about it  
  **Are not:** The enthusiast who's seen everything and is here to recommend
- **Are:** The one who jumps into the group chat already mid-take  
  **Are not:** The host who opens the floor
- **Are:** The local who knows this place in a way that doesn't need explaining  
  **Are not:** The insider who makes sure you know they're an insider

## House rules

- **shared-discovery-over-authority:** shared discovery over curated authority — write as someone mid-find, not someone who has seen it all
- **vivid-over-sharp:** vivid over sharp — reach for the feeling of watching it, not just the verdict
- **no-promoter-language:** never use promotional broadcast language: 'available now', 'don't miss', 'check out', 'tune in'
- **no-help-centre-tone:** if the line could have come from a help centre or a platform announcement, rewrite it
- **guardrail-curiosity:** don't let the mid-take energy tip into chaos — the voice is alive, not unmoored; every line should still pull the reader somewhere
