# LOOK — Hunch
*Artefact only. From The Book of WHO, plus the working values in the Hunchmail template v2. Every Hunch container wears this; the engine's chrome never does.*

**Font — headlines:** Bebas Neue, ALL CAPS. Headlines, subs and sub subs. `assets/BebasNeue-Regular.woff2` (400). Open Font License, `assets/BebasNeue-OFL.txt`. Fallback: Impact, Haettenschweiler, sans-serif.
**Font — body:** the web-safe stack, no file. Avenir Next, then Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial. 400 Regular and 600 Semibold, never 700.
**Logo:** the hunch wordmark. `assets/hunch-wordmark-red.svg` (preferred), `assets/hunch-wordmark-white.svg` (knocked out of red), `assets/hunch-wordmark-black.svg` (black-and-white documents only). Clear space is one X on every side, where X is the height of the h — the files are trimmed tight, so X is the artwork's own height.
**Mark:** the circle h, for when the space is small. `assets/hunch-h-red.svg` (solid red, white h) and `assets/hunch-h-black.svg` (black, for black-and-white documents). Vector, so it draws at any size.

**Red:** #ED1C24 (Hunch Red) — the only accent. 237/28/36, CMYK 0/100/100/0
**Ink:** #000000 (Black) — type, strokes, connectors
**Card:** #FFFFFF (White)

Working greys, off the live artefact:

**Ink body:** #202020 — body copy in email
**Ink soft:** #656565 — footers, supporting text
**Ground:** #ECEEF1 — panels inside the artefact
**Canvas:** #D4D3D3 — outside the artefact

**Devices:** the speech bubble carries a headline or a label and is best at two words, max. Line illustrations are black, white and Hunch Red only. Red is a scalpel, not a paintbrush: it marks the point, the payoff or the intersection, and never more than about a tenth of the picture.

Layout (widths, radii, type sizes) lives in each container's html, not here.
